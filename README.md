## 🚀 What I'm working on

**[Cuprio](https://cuprio.dev)**
The AI hardware engineer. Describe the circuit board you want in plain English, and a team of six AI engineers designs it, all the way to files you can send to a factory.

* Spec, Plan, Schematic, Layout, Routing and Review: each AI engineer owns one step
* They check each other's work with real rule-checking code, huddle when something won't fit, and ask you before anything changes
* Real parts, priced against live JLCPCB and LCSC stock
* Our own autorouter, in the browser: all 175 connections of a demo board in about 7 seconds
* A 3D view with the real parts, then the files to order it assembled from JLCPCB or PCBWay

We're in a closed beta, with 191 people on the waitlist.
